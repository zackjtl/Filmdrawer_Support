Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Filmdrawer is a photo organizer for Mac. It does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

  • Your photos stay on your Mac. Filmdrawer copies and organizes photos only into folders you choose on your own Mac. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
  • No account, no analytics, no tracking. Filmdrawer has no sign-in, contains no analytics or advertising code, and does not track you across apps or websites.
  • Settings stay local. Preferences such as your library location and language are stored only on your Mac.
  • In-app purchase. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Filmdrawer never sees your payment information.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.

Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.

プライバシーポリシー

最終更新日：2026 年 11 月 21 日

Filmdrawer は Mac 用の写真整理 App です。個人データを収集、保存、共有することは一切ありません。

  • 写真は Mac の中だけに。Filmdrawer は、ご自身の Mac 上で選んだフォルダに写真をコピー・整理するだけで、どこにもアップロードしません。
